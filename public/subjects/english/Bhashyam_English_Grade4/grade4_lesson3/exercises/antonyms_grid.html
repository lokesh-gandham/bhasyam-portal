🎮 How to Play

👉 Choose any word from the left side.
🔍 Find its opposite (opposite meaning) hidden in the letter grid.
👆 Click each letter of the hidden word one by one in the correct order.
✅ Click the Check button to see if your answer is correct.
🎨 If your answer is correct, the word will be highlighted with the same color as the question.
🏆 Find and complete all the hidden opposite words to finish the game.
