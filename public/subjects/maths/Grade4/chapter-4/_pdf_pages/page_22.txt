When we divide a number by 1000, the quotient is the number obtained by 
removing the digits in hundreds, tens and ones place (first three digits from right). 
The remainder will be the number formed by first three digits from the right.
1)  4589 ÷ 1000, 		
Q is 4 and R = 589
2)  20076 ÷ 1000,	
Q is 20 and R = 76
